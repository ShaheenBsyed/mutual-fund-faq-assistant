HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund Scheme Information Document

The Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) for the HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund (Direct Growth) are officially available here.

This fund was launched on June 25, 2007, with a launch NAV of ₹10.00. The fund is benchmarked against the NIFTY Midcap 150 Total Return Index (TRI).

The investment objective of the scheme is to provide long-term capital appreciation/income by investing predominantly in mid-cap companies.
