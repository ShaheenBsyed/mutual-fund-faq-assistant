HDFC Focused 30 Fund Scheme Information Document

The Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) for the HDFC Focused 30 Fund (Direct Growth) are officially available here.

This scheme was launched on September 17, 2004, with a launch NAV of ₹10.00. The fund is benchmarked against the NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI).

The investment objective of this scheme is to generate long-term capital appreciation by investing in a focused portfolio of up to 30 companies.
