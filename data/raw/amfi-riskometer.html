AMFI Riskometer Guidelines

The Riskometer is an educational tool introduced by SEBI/AMFI that visually represents the risk level of mutual fund schemes. It has 6 risk levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and Very High risk.

All five HDFC equity schemes (Mid-Cap Opportunities, Flexi Cap, Focused 30, ELSS Tax Saver, and Top 100) are categorized in the 'Very High Risk' level, denoting high equity exposure.
