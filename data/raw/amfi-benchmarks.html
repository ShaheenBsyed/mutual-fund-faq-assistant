AMFI Scheme Benchmark Guidelines

As per AMFI/SEBI guidelines, every mutual fund scheme must compare its performance against a target index known as the benchmark.

HDFC Top 100 Large Cap Fund is benchmarked against the NIFTY 100 TRI. HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is benchmarked against NIFTY Midcap 150 TRI. HDFC Flexi Cap, Focused 30, and ELSS Tax Saver are benchmarked against NIFTY 500 TRI.
