HDFC Mutual Fund exit load, expense ratio, and other fees

HDFC Mutual Fund charge structures: HDFC Mid-Cap, Flexi Cap, Focused 30, and Top 100 charge an exit load of 1% if redeemed or switched out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment. No exit load is charged if redeemed after 1 year.

The HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund has Nil exit load. However, the ELSS fund has a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years.

The Expense Ratio represents the management and operational fees charged by HDFC Asset Management Company for managing the funds, which ranges between 0.70% and 0.80% for direct plans.
