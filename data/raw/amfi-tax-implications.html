AMFI Mutual Fund Tax Implications Guide

Taxation on equity mutual funds in India:

Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) tax is 20% if equity units are redeemed within 12 months of allotment. Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax is 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year if equity units are redeemed after 12 months.
