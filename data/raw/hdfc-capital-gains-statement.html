How to Download HDFC Capital Gains Statement

To download your HDFC Capital Gains Statement for tax filing:

1. Log in to the HDFC Mutual Fund official portal. 2. Navigate to the 'Statements' or 'Service Requests' section. 3. Click on 'Capital Gains Statement'.

4. Select the relevant Financial Year, Folio Number, and click 'Download PDF' or 'Email Statement' to get your transaction tax report.
