AMFI ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) Guide

Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) is a category of mutual funds that offers tax savings under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

Under AMFI guidelines, ELSS funds have a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment. Redemptions, switches, or withdrawals are not permitted before the completion of 3 years.
