HDFC Flexi Cap Fund Scheme Information Document

The Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) for the HDFC Flexi Cap Fund / Equity Fund (Direct Growth) are officially available here.

This scheme was launched on January 1, 1995, with a launch NAV of ₹10.00. The fund is benchmarked against the NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI).

The investment objective is to generate long-term capital appreciation/income from a portfolio of equity and equity-related instruments across large cap, mid cap, and small cap stocks.
