How to Download HDFC Mutual Fund Account Statement

To download your HDFC Mutual Fund Account Statement:

1. Go to the HDFC MF Portal (www.hdfcfund.com) or use the 'HDFC MF Online' mobile app. 2. Log in using your PAN, Folio Number, or Username. 3. Navigate to 'Statements' -> 'Account Statement'.

4. Select the Folio, Scheme, and Date Range, then click 'Download PDF' or choose 'Email Me' to get the statement on your registered email address.
