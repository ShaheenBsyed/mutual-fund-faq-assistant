HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund Scheme Information Document

The Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) for the HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund (Direct Plan Growth) are officially available here.

This scheme was launched on December 10, 1999, with a launch NAV of ₹10.00. The fund is benchmarked against the NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI).

The lock-in period details: The fund has a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of investment. This is the shortest lock-in period among all Section 80C tax-saving options, making it a highly preferred choice.
